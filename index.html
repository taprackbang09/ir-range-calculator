Maximum range matrix
How max range is found. The size each drone presents at the chosen aspect is measured on its 3D model: projected area A and largest extent. The pixel limit is R_px = (f_mm × D_m) / (N × p_μm) km, with D = √A or the extent. The contrast limit R_snr is the farthest range where the simulated peak signal still clears the minimum SNR over NETD noise, including atmospheric loss τ = e−βR, diffraction blur and sub-pixel dilution. Max range = min(R_px, R_snr); the line under each value says which one limits it.
Default assumption: one pixel is enough to see the drone (N = 1 on √A, preset “Seen 1”). The drone counts as seen once its critical dimension fills one pixel and that pixel stands out from the noise by the minimum SNR. This is a sighting threshold for a hot, high-contrast target; it is more optimistic than Johnson detection (2 px), and recognition or identification need the R and I presets. Simulator model. Airframes are simplified 3D meshes built from the published dimensions above. Skin temperature = ambient + adiabatic recovery heating 0.85·v²/(2cp) at an assumed cruise speed (Г-3 ≈185 km/h, Г-4 ≈500, Г-5 ≈590, S8000 ≈610). Piston engine: cowling ≈80–120 °C, exhaust stubs ≈340 °C. Turbojet: tail section ≈70 °C, nozzle interior ≈520 °C (seen only from the rear hemisphere), plume optically thin in LWIR (it is much brighter in MWIR 3–5 µm). Background: clear night sky colder than ambient (−3 K at the horizon to −43 K at zenith), ground at ambient +1 K; no sun, clouds or terrain clutter. Sensor: in-band 8–14 µm Planck radiance, supersampled pixel-area integration, diffraction blur σ ≈ 0.45·λ·F#/p (λ = 10 µm), contrast transmission τ = e−βR, white NETD noise, linear AGC clipped at the 0.5 / 99.5 percentiles with a 2.5 K minimum span and a mild tone curve, so a hot nozzle saturates instead of crushing the airframe to black. Johnson levels use the critical dimension √(projected area), 50 % probability. A hot nozzle can still be detected below 2 px when its SNR is high; Johnson criteria describe extended targets. Orthographic projection; no turbulence, motion blur, fixed-pattern noise or NUC artefacts.
